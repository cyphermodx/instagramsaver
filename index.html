Reels downloader FAQ

Common questions about downloading Instagram reels.

Will the audio be included in my video download?

Yes. The video file keeps the reel's own audio, including music and voiceover. To save only the sound, switch to the Audio tab.

Are watermarks added to downloaded reels?

This site doesn't add its own watermark or branding to your file.

What video quality can I expect?

You get the quality the creator uploaded and Instagram makes available. We don't re-encode files, so some reels will be lower resolution than others.

Can I save just the audio without the video?

Yes. Choose Audio, paste the link, and download the reel's soundtrack or voiceover as an audio file.

What about reels from private accounts?

Private accounts aren't supported. Only public reels can be saved.

Save Instagram reels with the parts that matter

Reels depend on timing, vertical framing, and audio working together. This page saves public reels in a format that stays useful outside the Instagram app.

Why use a reel-specific saver

A reel isn't just another video post. Short-form pacing, music cues, captions, and vertical framing all matter, so a weak download can strip away the parts that made the reel useful.

Good reasons to save a reel

  • Back up your own reels before editing, deleting, or reposting them elsewhere.
  • Review hooks, pacing, subtitles, and audio choices from public examples.
  • Keep tutorials, explainers, or campaign references available offline.

Choose the output that matches the job

If the reel itself matters, save the full video. If you only need the soundtrack, voiceover, or a spoken clip, use audio-only.

Before you download

  • Use video for edit reference, repeat planning, or archive.
  • Use audio only when sound matters more than visuals.
  • Copy the link directly from Instagram. Trimmed or tracking-heavy links can fail.

Reuse reels responsibly

Downloading a public reel doesn't transfer ownership. If you plan to share, publish, or repurpose it, credit and permission still matter.

Good practice

  • Credit the original creator when sharing public clips.
  • Don't repost private, paid, or closed-community content outside its intended audience.
  • Ask for permission before commercial reuse when the reel isn't yours.
